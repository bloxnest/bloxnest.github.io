A few quick answers

How do I open two Roblox windows at once?

Finish your game, open BloxNest, choose 2 windows and start them. Use a different account in each window to play with multiple accounts at once.

Can I get banned?

BloxNest doesn’t modify Roblox or inject anything. It isn’t made or approved by Roblox, so nobody can promise you won’t be banned; use it at your own risk.

Is BloxNest free?

Yes, BloxNest is free to use. Its code is source available under the PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0: you can read the code and use the app, but you can’t redistribute it or publish modified versions.
